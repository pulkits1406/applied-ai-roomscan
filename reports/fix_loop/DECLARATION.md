# Fix-loop declaration

**Scope, stated up front.** Part 4 asks for the single worst-performing gate *in our own benchmark*.
No benchmark capture exists (no iPhone), so that requirement is **BLOCKED**. On the data we do have,
the worst results are the photo and video wall gates (0 % within ±8 % / ±3 % on pseudo-GT,
`reports/benchmark_report.md` §1); we did not fix them, and we do not present them as fixed. What
follows is the strongest complete diagnose → fix → regenerable before/after cycle we shipped: a
LiDAR room-geometry failure measured against **exact synthetic ground truth**. It is not benchmark
evidence, and this declaration was written **after** the fix was shipped (2026-10-05, commit
`8570bb4`); the prediction below is the one recorded in the engineering log at that time, not a
blind pre-registration.

## 1. Failing measurement and number

Synthetic two-room flat (`src/roomscan/synthetic.py`; room A 4.0 × 3.0 m, room B 2.5 × 3.0 m,
exact geometry), LiDAR tier, scored by the production evaluator (`site/site.yaml`):
both rooms' 3.0 m dimension came out as **2.50 m and 2.00 m (−499 mm, −1004 mm)**, floor areas
**−16.7 % and −33.6 %**, the affected walls labelled `inferred`, and their intervals (±17 cm) did
**not** cover the error. On a real capture the same defect would remove whole walls of rooms along
the scan's outer edge — it is a wall-dimension and room-topology failure, the quantity that the
repeatability and per-tier wall gates are built on. (Numbers: `RESULTS.md`, regenerated by `run.sh`.)

## 2. Root cause and evidence

`geometry.segment_rooms` closes the observed-floor domain with a 0.6 m morphological closing.
scipy's erosion treats everything outside the grid as empty, and the grid had only 0.3 m of margin
below the data and **none above**, so rooms were eroded near the grid border.
Evidence (e22/synthetic diagnosis, `ENGINEERING_LOG.md` "Synthetic capture + smoke test"):
- floor observed up to z = 3.02 m, yet the room-A region ended at z = 2.47 m (0.53 m inset at the
  high edge, 0.27 m at the low edge) with no barrier cells in between;
- `room_cloud` crops points to the region + 0.5 m, i.e. z ≤ 2.97 m: the north wall at 3.00 m was
  cut away by 3 cm, so no wall line existed there and the ladder inferred one at the floor boundary;
- consistent with the arithmetic: closing radius 0.6 m − margin 0.3 m ≈ 0.27–0.3 m inset; 0.6 m − 0
  ≈ 0.53–0.6 m inset (5 cm cells).

## 3. Fix and predicted result

Pad the segmentation grid by more than the closing radius on both sides (1.0 m), keeping v0's
defaults unchanged so the frozen baseline stays byte-identical (`fix.diff`: `segment_rooms` gains
`pad_m`/`pad_high_m`; LiDAR v1 passes 1.0/1.0). **Predicted:** both rooms recovered with all four
walls measured (no `inferred` faces) and wall errors at the synthetic noise floor (a few mm, as for
the unaffected walls before the fix, −1.1 to −4.8 mm); no change on real captures whose rooms are
not near the grid border.

## 4. Result (shipped; regenerate with `reports/fix_loop/run.sh`)

| | before (`--seg-pad 0.3 0.0`) | after (default) |
|---|---|---|
| walls within 1 cm of truth | 4 of 8 | **8 of 8** (−1.9 … −2.2 mm) |
| worst wall error | −1004 mm | −2.2 mm |
| room areas | −16.7 %, −33.6 % | −0.12 %, −0.16 % |
| inferred walls / flags | 7 inferred, `r1/r2_walls_inferred` | none |
| supplied capture (no-regression) | — | structure identical, max numeric difference 0.36 mm (`results/real_capture_before_vs_after.json`) |

The prediction held. Post-mortem: the before-state's intervals for inferred walls were far too
narrow for a border-erosion failure (that motivated the later stability term, `src/roomscan/quality.py`).

## 5. Regenerability and diff

- `reports/fix_loop/run.sh` renders the synthetic capture, runs **before** (`roomscan-lidar --seg-pad
  0.3 0.0`, the exact pre-fix segmentation) and **after** with the same code through
  `roomscan-bench --execute`, copies the results, and repeats the comparison on the supplied capture.
- `fix.diff` = `git diff 5b25c37 8570bb4 -- src/roomscan/geometry.py src/roomscan/lidar/rooms.py`
  (the shipped fix). The pre-fix code itself is also regenerable from tag history (`git checkout 5b25c37`).

## What this bundle does not show

A failing **assessment gate on a real benchmark** moving to pass. That needs the laser benchmark
(`docs/physical_benchmark_procedure.md`); candidates already identified for it: doorway width
1 cm-bin bias (+10 mm on synthetic, within the 2 cm gate but systematic), bimodal ceilings
(room 3 flips 104 mm between halves), inter-visit misregistration (30–160 mm raw).
